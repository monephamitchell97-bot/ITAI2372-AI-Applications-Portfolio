# AI in Manufacturing

## Project Overview

This project explores how artificial intelligence can improve manufacturing operations through automation, equipment monitoring, quality control, and data-driven decision-making.

## AI Applications

- Predictive maintenance
- Automated quality inspection
- Robotics and process automation
- Supply-chain optimization
- Production scheduling
- Workplace safety monitoring

## Benefits

AI can help manufacturers reduce equipment downtime, improve product quality, increase productivity, reduce waste, and lower operating costs.

## Risks and Challenges

- Cybersecurity threats
- High implementation costs
- Employee training needs
- Job displacement concerns
- Incorrect or biased AI decisions
- Overdependence on automated systems

## Key Learning

AI can improve manufacturing efficiency and safety by detecting problems early and supporting faster decisions. Successful implementation requires employee training, strong cybersecurity, reliable data, and continued human oversight.
