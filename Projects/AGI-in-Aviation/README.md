# AGI in Aviation

## Project Overview

This project explores how Artificial General Intelligence could be applied within the aviation industry to improve safety, maintenance, flight operations, emergency response, scheduling, and decision-making.

## Problem Addressed

The aviation industry relies on large amounts of information from aircraft systems, maintenance records, weather reports, airport operations, and safety programs. Processing this information quickly and accurately can be difficult.

## Proposed AGI Applications

- Analyze maintenance records and identify possible equipment problems
- Combine weather, flight, and airport information to support safer decisions
- Assist with emergency planning and operational responses
- Improve scheduling, resource management, and customer service
- Support aviation safety professionals with data-driven recommendations

## Risks and Ethical Concerns

- Cybersecurity threats
- Privacy concerns
- Incorrect or unreliable decisions
- Accountability for AGI actions
- Overdependence on automation

## Key Learning

AGI could transform aviation by connecting information from several systems and supporting complex decisions. However, human oversight, strong cybersecurity, continuous testing, and clear regulations would remain essential.
