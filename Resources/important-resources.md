# Important AI Learning Resources

This page contains professional resources that supported my understanding of artificial intelligence, responsible AI, aviation, government, and manufacturing applications.

## Responsible Artificial Intelligence

### NIST AI Risk Management Framework

The NIST AI Risk Management Framework provides guidance for identifying, evaluating, and managing risks associated with artificial intelligence systems.

https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework

### NIST AI Resource Center

The NIST AI Resource Center provides tools and information for applying responsible and trustworthy AI practices.

https://airc.nist.gov/

## AI in Government

### U.S. Government Accountability Office AI Accountability Framework

This resource explains important practices for responsible, transparent, and accountable use of AI in government programs.

https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-21-519sp

## AI in Aviation

### Federal Aviation Administration: Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning

This FAA resource discusses the evaluation and certification of AI and machine-learning systems used in aviation.

https://www.faa.gov/aircraft/air_cert/step/disciplines/artificial_intelligence

### FAA Next Generation Air Transportation System

This resource explains how advanced technologies and data-driven systems are being used to modernize the National Airspace System.

https://www.faa.gov/nextgen

## AI in Manufacturing

### NIST: Artificial Intelligence in U.S. Manufacturing

This resource discusses how artificial intelligence can support manufacturing processes, productivity, and decision-making.

https://www.nist.gov/mep/manufacturing-infographics/rise-artificial-intelligence-us-manufacturing

### NIST Augmented Intelligence for Manufacturing Systems

This resource examines how artificial intelligence, measurement systems, and operational data can improve manufacturing quality and performance.

https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/augmented-intelligence-manufacturing-systems-aims

## Key Learning

These resources helped me understand that successful AI applications require reliable data, proper testing, cybersecurity, ethical standards, accountability, and human oversight.
