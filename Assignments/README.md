# Selected Course Assignments

This folder contains selected assignments completed for **ITAI 2372 – AI Applications**. These projects demonstrate my ability to research artificial intelligence technologies, evaluate their benefits and risks, and explain real-world applications.

## Assignment Highlights

### AI in Hiring and Recruitment

This assignment examines how artificial intelligence can assist with candidate screening, applicant matching, and recruitment decisions. It also considers concerns involving bias, fairness, privacy, and human oversight.

### AI and Blockchain Integration

This assignment explores how artificial intelligence and blockchain can work together to improve security, automation, transparency, and data management.

### AI in Media and Entertainment

This assignment discusses how AI is used for content recommendations, audience analysis, content creation, personalization, and production support.

### AI in Fraud Detection

This case study examines how Mastercard Decision Intelligence uses AI to analyze transactions, detect unusual patterns, and help prevent payment fraud.
