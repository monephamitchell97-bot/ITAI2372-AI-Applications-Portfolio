# Artificial Intelligence Across Industries

Artificial intelligence can help organizations analyze data, automate tasks, identify patterns, and make better decisions.

## Aviation

AI can support predictive maintenance, safety analysis, flight planning, airport operations, and customer service.

## Manufacturing

AI can improve automated quality control, equipment monitoring, robotics, production scheduling, and supply-chain management.

## Government

AI can support fraud detection, emergency response, public health, infrastructure planning, and citizen services.

## Cybersecurity

AI can help identify unusual activity, detect threats, assess risks, monitor networks, and support incident response.

## Key Learning

AI applications should be selected based on a clear problem or organizational need. Successful AI solutions require reliable data, employee training, cybersecurity, ethical review, and human oversight.
