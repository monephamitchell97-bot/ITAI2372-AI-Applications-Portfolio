# Responsible Artificial Intelligence

Responsible AI refers to designing and using artificial intelligence in a safe, fair, transparent, and accountable way.

## Important Principles

- Fairness: AI systems should not unfairly discriminate against people.
- Transparency: Organizations should explain how AI systems are used.
- Privacy: Personal and sensitive information must be protected.
- Accountability: People and organizations must remain responsible for AI decisions.
- Reliability: AI systems should be tested for accuracy and consistency.
- Human Oversight: Important decisions should include human review.

## Key Learning

AI can provide significant benefits, but it can also create risks involving bias, privacy, cybersecurity, and incorrect decisions. Responsible AI requires regular testing, strong security, clear policies, and continued human oversight.
